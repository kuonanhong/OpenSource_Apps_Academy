vod2pod-rss

vod2pod-rss 是用於訂閱來源與新聞閱讀、Podcast 或有聲內容管理的應用。

Convert YouTube and Twitch channels to podcasts, no storage required. Transcodes VoDs to MP3 192k on the fly, generates an RSS feed to use in podcast clients.
