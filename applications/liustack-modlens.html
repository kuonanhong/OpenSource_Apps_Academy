modlens

為 DeepSeek Harness 等以文字為主的程式智能體提供視覺橋接，把圖片輸入轉成 OCR、版面與語義觀察等結構化證據。辨識品質及可用服務依視覺後端設定而定。

A vision bridge for DeepSeek Harness and other text-oriented coding agents, turning image input into structured evidence such as OCR, layout and semantic observations. Recognition quality and available providers depend on the configured vision backend.
