needle

Needle 提供裝置端推論引擎與小型模型，用於工具選擇、結構化擷取和文字嵌入，並可搭配語音模型輸入音訊。硬體支援、模型下載與權重授權需另外確認。

Needle provides an on-device inference engine and small models for tool selection, structured extraction and embeddings; its companion speech model can add audio input. Hardware support, model downloads and weight licences require separate checks.

Apache-2.0
