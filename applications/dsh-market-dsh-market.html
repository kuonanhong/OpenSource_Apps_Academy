dsh-market

在 DeepSeek Harness 設定頁內提供可搜尋的插件市場、插件說明與安裝控制。市場程式本身的授權，不代表每個第三方插件都具有相同授權或安全性。

Embeds a searchable plugin marketplace in DeepSeek Harness settings, with plugin details and installation controls. The marketplace software licence does not establish the licence or safety of every listed third-party plugin.
