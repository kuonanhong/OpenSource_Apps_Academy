Kokoro

82M 參數的開放權重文字轉語音模型，可把導讀合成可下載音訊；官方範例包含英、日、華語與數種歐洲及印度語言。

An 82-million-parameter open-weight text-to-speech model for downloadable lesson narration in its supported languages.

Apache-2.0 for the inference library and official Kokoro-82M weights
